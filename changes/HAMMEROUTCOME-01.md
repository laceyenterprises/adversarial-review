# HAMMEROUTCOME-01

The closer emits a run-bound exact-head GitHub merge receipt only after its own
successful merge execution. Terminal phases are retained separately from progress
credit; remediation-pushed includes the new head. Every hammer-ended-without-merge
event carries a closed phaseOutcome and raw lastPhase, including unclassified.
These diagnostic receipts grant no merge authority and do not change retry policy.
Agent OS harvests the terminal outcome into worker-run metadata and owns reporting
and existing ticket alert staging. CFG mirrors the two burn thresholds with defaults
6 runs / 120000000 tokens. All fixtures are synthetic.
