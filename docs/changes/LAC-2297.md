# LAC-2297 — Retire a canonically fenced legacy repair epoch

## Summary

The native repair reservation keeps custody across failed, filtered and
point-in-time absence reads. LAC-2297 adds an HQ-owner operation that can
retire one unscoped legacy merge-rescue reservation with the exact committed
enqueue fence from [Agent OS #11350](https://github.com/laceyenterprises/agent-os/pull/11350).

## Interface changes

`bin/retire-legacy-repair.mjs --apply` takes the same repo, PR, full base head,
epoch, owner key and original handoff round as the producer. It reads the
durable receipt through the canonical Postgres read-only runtime, verifies the
enqueue guards and checks the owner family for launches and retries. The
producer's evidence builder verifies the current open PR, exact reservation
and original submitter under AR's intent and retry-ledger locks. The operation
records `canonical-enqueue-fenced` and the receipt on the terminal epoch.
Competing custody, queued work and all spend and refund history stay intact.

The existing dispatch-read grant authorizes an observation of launch state or
absence. The HQ-owner boundary already authorizes the canonical proof read, so
the command needs no additional entitlement. The sibling watcher, reserve,
next-action and TTM paths continue to use pending versus bound or terminal
repair custody. The [runbook](../RUNBOOK-ama-closure.md#retire-one-fenced-legacy-repair-reservation-lac-2297)
owns operator acceptance after deployment.

## Verification

The offline retirement fixtures exercise CLI selector binding, absence
refusal, committed receipt acceptance, conflicting identity and custody,
shared-lock contention, a concurrent queue arrival and exact history
preservation. The read-only bridge fixtures exercise operator and canonical
backend checks, the unfiltered producer read, committed receipt visibility
and mismatches in current PR, reservation and handoff evidence.

- `node test/run-tests.mjs retire-legacy-repair.test.mjs` passed all 31
  tests, including the five read-only bridge cases. Targeted ESLint and
  Ruff checks passed.
- `node test/run-tests.mjs repairdispatchread-01.test.mjs
  inplacerepair-01.test.mjs watcher-claim-loop.test.mjs` accounted for 278
  passing tests in the combined repair and watcher validation run.
- The accepted producer's `test_repair_enqueue_fence.py -k sqlite` passed seven
  tests, including 20 enqueue-first and 20 fence-first races. Six
  backend-specific cases skipped. The worker's disposable Postgres connection
  was denied at its local socket.
