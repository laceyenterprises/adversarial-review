## Summary

A later unproven head reaches one verdict-only review through the
exhausted-head admission path. This ticket adds a regression and documents the
boundary. The available evidence does not justify a runtime policy change.

## Admission and recovery

`findFinalRemediationPushJob` matches the live SHA to both
`completion.workerPushedHeadSha` and `reReview.nextAction.head` in native
state. The watcher retains that endpoint for closure and evaluates the
ordinary closure checks. `createReviewCurrencyReader` can admit the older
review's findings through ancestry. Exact-head matching, authenticated
publication, preservation of primary source, and current required CI have
their own checks. Independent findings and later review or operator vetoes
continue to apply.

At exhaustion, `admitExhaustedHeadVerdict` checks the live head for closer
ownership and an already delivered verdict before arming
`system-exhausted-head-verdict:<sha>`. The claim also respects native merge
custody. Exhaustion ends the ordinary remediation cycle. The banner's `5 of 3`
is consistent with four recorded completed remediation rounds and an
effective cap of two. It puts no launch count or request origin on the record.
An explicit exact-head request or another recovery path has its own reason
and can use the same final prompt.

To establish an unintended reopen, obtain the watcher claim and request
reason, live SHA and reviewer pass record for the supplied session, plus the
completed job's exact `nextAction.head`. If the claim was at the later
rescue SHA, identify authenticated closure publication evidence covering
that endpoint and available before the claim, or an existing delivered verdict
for it. Check ownership, intent and lease observations at admission. For
future rescue work, publish remediation-only changes via the existing native
closure path so it can record the resulting head. Put unrelated primary
changes through an exact-head verdict, or deliver them separately. An
operator classification of rescue scope can guide that choice, and the
existing proof and CI gates still decide admission and merge.

LAC-2331 covers native replay and Hammer rebase currency. LAC-2332 covers
deferred closer source custody. Neither substitutes for evidence binding a
later arbitrary rescue publication.
