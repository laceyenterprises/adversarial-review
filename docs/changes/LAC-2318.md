## Summary

The fix-all wrapper now uses the same final publication proof as Comment-only
closeout. The native HQ path binds the canonical worker workspace and verified
produced attestation to the exact destination head. The Git proof checks replay
of the reviewed patches and the provenance of additional commits. Native
publication may supply the exact tip's identity through its produced attestation.
Every source advisory needs a recorded fix. The original review and its findings
are retained for closer validation. The independent native source, history,
cost, CI, owner and operator decisions still govern merge.

The CI repair moves completion metadata assembly into the closeout module.
The watcher replay starts from a completed native receipt and verifies that the
closer receives the original findings and published head.

## Interface changes

A completed native fix-all receipt includes `producedAttestationId` in its
structural `completion.workerPushProof`. The outcome comment shows the
one-round work order and the PR-wide round number. Its audit line keeps total
spend and the ordinary budget visible. Queue, retry and spend limits are the same.

Transient attestation read failures use the existing final-proof retry schedule
and one-hour reconciliation window. Missing, invalid or permanently unreadable
attestations still withhold publication proof. The operator-only CI decline
leaves the advisory unresolved and allows fallback review admission.

At rollout, a completed pre-deploy fix-all receipt without native proof can
lose its closer handoff. An open PR still waiting on that receipt may receive
one fallback review of the current head under ordinary admission.
