# LAC-2307 — LIVEPACKHANDOFF-01: live build pack findings keep operator custody

## Summary

Remediators may not edit a live build pack's `projects/<slug>/SPEC.md`,
`SPEC.meta.json`, `plan.json` or `prompts/**` (REMEDIATIONLIVEPLAN-01). The
handoff for a valid finding that needs such an edit was untruthful. Native
remediators were told to file it as `pushback[]` with
`Resolved-By-Policy: live-pack-plan <path> requires an operator live edit` and
"no blocker, no operator decision". The hammer's `bin/guard-forbidden-paths.mjs`
then counted the same finding as remediated in its audit. Two retained witnesses
show the result. A final blocking round (2 of 2) put a valid SPEC finding under
"Pushback (won't fix)", and the next owner was CI. A comment-only round called two
valid prompt findings "a policy deferral, not a request for human input", and the
next owner was the hammer. In both cases nothing kept an accountable operator
owner, and only a manual rescue repaired the pack files.

The protected-source policy is unchanged: no worker edits those files, and the
`live-pack-edit-approved` label is not a worker write grant. The handoff is now
truthful:

- **Native remediator.** The rule in `src/remediation-prompt-builder.mjs`, the
  Trusted Live Build Pack Facts rule (`src/live-pack-cross-edit.mjs`) and the
  three code-pr stage prompts now say the same thing. Record the finding once in
  `blockers[]` with `kind: "live-pack-operator-edit"` and
  `needsHumanInput: "Operator-Live-Edit-Required: live-pack-plan <path>"`, even
  when it is non-blocking. Then set `reReview.requested = false`.
- **Reply kernel.** `validateRemediationReply` moves any live-pack deferral out of
  `pushback[].reasoning`, `addressed[].action` or `nonBlocking[].action`. A
  deferral is `Resolved-By-Policy: live-pack-plan`,
  `Operator-Live-Edit-Required: live-pack-plan`, or "requires an operator live
  edit", unless negated. Each one becomes that tagged blocker. A `completed`
  outcome becomes `partial`, and `reReview.requested` is cleared. Every consumer
  (final-round completion, blocker ownership, PR comments, finding records)
  therefore sees human ownership, and misleading "complete" text cannot finish the
  round. Quoted reviewer wording in an evidence-backed factual dispute is not
  an affirmative deferral. A tagged blocker whose title matches only a non-blocking finding is kept
  out of the blocking-coverage count. Evidence-backed disputes and other pushback
  are unchanged.
- **Closer.** The helper now reports such findings in `operatorOwned`, with
  `Operator-Live-Edit-Required` lines in `operatorHandoff`. They are not in
  `resolved` and earn no audit credit. Its instructions tell the hammer what to do
  while a required entry remains (blocking always; non-blocking under strict
  mode). It must not merge, and must stop with `blocking-findings-present` or
  `non-blocking-findings-present`. Both are existing deterministic world-fact
  stops that hold and page once per head. Appendix B of the hammer prompt says the
  findings stay operator-owned.
  Mixed remedies retain ordinary remediation and operator custody together.
  Proof of the protected edit alone cannot count the other action as addressed.
- **Exact-head operator repair.** `src/live-pack-operator-edit.mjs` reads the
  evidence the helper uses to credit an operator's repair. Credit requires all of
  the following:
  - The PR is open, and its live head descends from `HAM_REVIEWED_SHA`. The
    compare status must be `ahead`, with complete commit and file lists.
  - Each protected path changed in that range, and only through commits that
    carry no `Worker-Class`, `Worker-Job-Id`, `Worker-Ticket` or `Closed-By`
    trailer.
  - The latest event for `live-pack-edit-approved` is a `labeled` by a
    `roles.adversarial.operator_logins` login.
  - The head and labels are unchanged after the read.

  Any failed, truncated or ambiguous read leaves the finding operator-owned. A
  proven entry moves to `resolved[].operatorEdit` with a
  `verified operator edit of <path> at exact head <sha>` audit bullet. The hammer
  still confirms that the edit satisfies the finding.
- **Predicate backstop.** `parseHamAuditFindingsFromBody`
  (`src/ama/dispatch-closer.mjs`) skips a live-pack deferral bullet. A hammer
  that copies legacy "resolved by policy; ... requires an operator live edit"
  prose therefore fails the remediated-count and identity gates. Generated-path
  (REVIEWGUARD-01) policy bullets keep their credit.

## Interface changes

- `src/kernel/remediation-reply.mjs`: new exports `LIVE_PACK_OPERATOR_EDIT_KIND`,
  `LIVE_PACK_OPERATOR_EDIT_MARKER` and `isLivePackDeferralText`. Validation
  normalizes live-pack deferrals into `blockers[]` entries.
  `RemediationReplyBlocker` gains an optional `kind: 'live-pack-operator-edit'`.
- `src/guard-forbidden-paths.mjs`: `resolveGuardForbiddenNonBlocking` returns
  `operatorOwned` and `operatorHandoff`. Live-pack findings are no longer in
  `resolved` or `auditDetails`; mixed findings also stay in `remaining` or
  `remainingBlocking`, and
  `LIVE_PACK_POLICY_INSTRUCTIONS` is rewritten.
- `bin/guard-forbidden-paths.mjs`: reads `HAM_REVIEWED_SHA`. When findings are
  operator-owned, it reads exact-head evidence through `gh api` and adds
  `operatorEditEvidence` to its output.
- New `src/live-pack-operator-edit.mjs`: `readOperatorLivePackEditEvidence` and
  `applyOperatorLivePackEdits`.
- `src/live-pack-cross-edit.mjs`: new export `LIVE_PACK_OPERATOR_HANDOFF`.

The change adds no config key, CI check, gate, ratchet, label or stop reason. It
makes no runtime, ledger, lease or pack change. Required checks, the live-pack
merge preflight, head binding and every other merge safety gate are unchanged.
Canonical docs were corrected in place:

- `docs/SPEC-adversarial-review-auto-remediation.md`: the label contract and the
  reply invariants.
- `docs/RUNBOOK-ama-closure.md`: strict non-blocking remediation.
- `docs/RUNBOOK-pr-control-labels.md`: the `live-pack-edit-approved` row.
