# LAC-2214 — CLI resolution retry custody

## Summary

Confirmed a mismatch between transient settlement and the independent hard
review-attempt ceiling. Settlement did not charge `review_attempts` for
`cli-resolution-transient`, but `countReviewCeilingAttempts` charged every failed
pass-journal entry with that class. When retry admission uses rereview safety
gates, the watcher could claim the pending row and immediately release it at
the hard attempt ceiling without starting a reviewer.

Exclude only failed CLI-resolution passes from that ceiling, alongside the
existing pre-launch infrastructure exclusions. Ordinary failures, running
passes, cancellations and completions keep their accounting. Durable cascade
backoff, claim CAS, reviewer selection and merge gates are unchanged.

Both had seven failed Claude first-pass journal records with
`failureClass=cli-resolution-transient`, no delivered review and zero
`review_attempts`. The cascade counter plateaued at five and a 15-minute delay;
the per-class breakdown retained all seven failures. The reported 01:24:26/33
timestamps were claim times, not the failure instants that anchor the deadline.
The owner-lane resolver's 01:25:49 installed fallback success was supplied by
the incident report; this investigation did not assume continued CLI failure.

Logs selected Gemini with `always-on-third-reviewer` and showed repeated
`hard review attempt ceiling reached (7 reviewer attempts >= 5)` followed by
`Released reviewer claim ... after hard-review-attempt-ceiling`.
The 01:44:02.937Z / 01:44:03.572Z exact-head operator requests returned
`already-pending` while recording retrigger intent. Later logs honored the
operator marker past the ceiling, claimed at 01:50:37.048Z / 01:50:46.575Z,
and persisted Gemini sessions. At observation both exact-head journal passes
were running. This establishes resumed custody after operator recovery, not
first-review delivery or deployed acceptance of this source fix.

The admitted Gemini candidates logged queue waits of 394,094 ms and 402,987 ms.
`nextRetryAfter` is earliest retry eligibility, not a reviewer-start deadline:
capacity and preflight still apply. Rereview safety gates use the discovery
snapshot, including `rereview_requested_at` and the operator marker; an
`already-pending` response records intent rather than synchronously starting
execution. The observed claim/release refusals followed by marker-bypassed
starts explain why that response alone was insufficient proof of recovery.
The transient cadence was bounded; the separate ceiling mismatch prevented
progress until the operator bypass was observed.

No live rows, roles, credentials or services were changed. Current main
`c8dcf8caf` includes PR #1920 and LAC-2213's reviewed rename correction
(#1921); neither guard is duplicated or changed.
